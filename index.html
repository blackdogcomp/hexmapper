How to

Pick a terrain, then click or drag to paint.
Fill floods a whole area of the same terrain.
Pick a place and click a hex to put it there.
Label: click a hex to name it.
River / Road: drag from hex to hex to draw one. Right-click a hex to lift it off; Erase clears it too.
Right-click (other tools) removes a hex's place and label.
Pan: right-drag or Space+drag · Zoom: wheel.
Autosaves in this browser.
